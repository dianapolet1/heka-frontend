Universe Of Pau

I am a weaver of symbols, a keeper of memory, and a believer in living magic. My work is born from dialogue between earth and cosmos, from the way wax holds time, fabric carries stories, and symbols speak louder than words.

Every piece i make carries intention; to awaken archetypes, to protect, to transform, to remind us of our divine essence. “As above so bellow, as within so without.” .
